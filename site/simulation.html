Keep money, rights, and quality separate

Operator cash is not member welfare. The purchasing cost difference compares modeled purchasing costs with direct purchasing and includes outstanding buyer liabilities, not just paid cash. Positive values mean lower modeled cost; late payments do not create savings. Restricted commons funds and contributor agency balances are not unrestricted operating income. Permission is a gate, not a price: more budget does not create contribution or repair authority.

Quality is the observed fulfilled/attempted task rate, not a promise. Read it beside denied and unmet demand: lower spending can accompany reduced service, and cost differences alone do not establish greater benefit. A missing quality rate means no valid denominator, not zero quality.
