Different meanings of break-even

Cash break-even starts the first six consecutive nonnegative cash-profit months. Meeting the target also requires the final six months to stay nonnegative. Run-rate profit removes expansion acquisition/onboarding and hiring, smooths events, and retains replacement acquisition/onboarding.

Read the assumptions & accounting boundary

A paid cohort starts only when the minimum launch size and after-fee saving requirement are met. Otherwise membership and fees are zero while the staffing and fixed-cost plan continues. This price check assumes equivalent service, no member-paid switching cost, and a provider able to serve the cohort. It does not test retention behavior, service quality, willingness to pay, or real customer acquisition.

Payroll is fully loaded, not take-home pay. Setup excludes employee payroll. The projection omits taxes, debt, interest, investor-return requirements, and a financing transaction schedule. Potential capital recovery is a cash-capacity check, not a scheduled distribution or guaranteed investor repayment.
