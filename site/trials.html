What these trials are, and are not

These are local JavaScript startup-finance calculations, not operating-agent runs, transactions, independent human observations or observed market outcomes. They do not call paid LLMs. A conditional trial share is not an empirical success probability. Capital covers a projected funding gap; it does not cause the planned member growth.

Design: ten profiles, kept separate

Each profile changes only the listed inputs from the balanced reference. Do not pool their outcomes into one success estimate: these are different financial choices evaluated with paired random draws.

Balanced reference and profile interpretation

{{REFERENCE_INPUTS}}

“Member value first” lowers the service fee and increases committed capital. “Balanced” means the unmodified reference, not an optimized or validated business plan. Ramp variants also change the cash break-even target as listed. The complete input record contains every parameter, including unchanged procurement, staffing and spending assumptions.

Holdout: a separate seed, not new evidence from people

Only member value first and balanced were carried forward from design. The holdout seed differs from the design seed while profile inputs and the financial method stay fixed. This checks sensitivity to another pseudorandom sample under the same assumptions. It is not independent market validation, preregistered selection or validation of the other eight profiles.

How much capital did the holdout plans require?

Bins count actual per-trial capital requirements, including the operating reserve and contingency. Each bin includes its lower bound and excludes its upper bound. Bar widths are relative to the largest bin within each profile, not a shared probability scale. All counts are available as text without color, interaction or JavaScript.

{{HISTOGRAMS}}

The median and P95 use linear interpolation at sorted index (n - 1) * p. P95 is a percentile of these declared assumptions, not a guarantee of sufficient capital. Unrounded cent-denominated quantiles are in the aggregate JSON; interpolation can produce fractional cents even though each trial's money is recorded in whole cents.

Method, outcomes and limits

Declared input distributions

{{DISTRIBUTIONS}}

A single cost multiplier is shared across the modeled cost categories in each trial. Procurement price and service-equivalence assumptions are not empirically calibrated by this study.

Seed independence and pairing

{{SEEDS}}

Draws use counter-keyed xoshiro128** with a startup:replication:factor key. Within a phase, profiles reuse the same seed and replication keys: comparisons are paired, not independent profile samples. Holdout is seed-independent of design only in this pseudorandom sampling sense.

“All goals met” requires every condition

  1. No projected cash shortfall, including the monthly pre-revenue cash check.
  2. Committed capital meets the maximum funding requirement plus operating reserve and contingency.
  3. Cash break-even starts by the profile's target month: six consecutive months of nonnegative operating cash profit, with the final six months also nonnegative.
  4. Member price protection: {{PRICE_GOAL}}. At least one paying cohort must exist.

“No cash break-even” means no six-month qualifying sequence within the horizon, not an execution failure. Cash break-even is not cumulative repayment of startup costs or an investor-return calculation. A qualifying early sequence alone does not guarantee all later months remain profitable.

Completeness and exclusions

{{COMPLETENESS}}

Generation aborts on a projection or serialization error. The publication build rejects missing, duplicate, out-of-order, stale or mismatched records and counts. Unfavorable outcomes are retained. Null break-even results are included in the goal and capital denominators; only the optional break-even-month quantiles in the downloadable aggregates exclude them, and report their remaining count.

Not a prediction of viability

The model applies an assumed member ramp and spending plan even if capital is insufficient. It does not estimate demand, causal growth from funding, service quality, general migration safety or nonmonetary welfare. Staff costs, acquisition costs and growth are uncalibrated. Taxes, debt service, required investor returns and legally approved capital repayment are not modeled.

The original aggregates retain Wilson Monte Carlo sampling intervals conditional on the declared distributions. Those intervals do not measure real-world business uncertainty and do not correct profile selection or model misspecification. This page reports conditional counts and shares, not investment confidence.

This frozen study is separate from the editable Simulation Lab and its current Follow the Money reference. No live edits silently change these published trials.

Where the data is stored

The repository stores this study in agentic-model/experiments/viability-2026-09-20/. Only the explicitly listed study artifacts are copied into this website's study-data/ directory. Other model folders and repository-only documents are not part of this download.

  • trial-evidence.json (validated presentation aggregates and histogram bins)

The funded, no-capital and member-value-first plan files are separate nominal projections with full monthly financial rows, not extra sampled trials or transaction/event traces. They supply optional frozen source profiles for Follow the Money. Its default reference is generated separately from the current Simulation Lab configuration.

Raw means per-trial summaries here. The gzipped UTF-8 JSON Lines file has one completed projection per line, with phase, profile, replication, seed reference, realized factors, financial summary and outcome flags. These cohort projections have no operating-agent transaction or event traces. The archive does not contain full monthly trajectories.

To reproduce in a checkout with Node.js 22 or later, run node scripts/analyze-startup.mjs, then node --test test/trial-evidence.test.mjs. Run the normal website build to publish validated artifacts. Gzip timestamps are zeroed; identical JSONL and compatible Node/zlib versions yield deterministic archives. A different compression implementation may change compressed bytes without changing decompressed records.
